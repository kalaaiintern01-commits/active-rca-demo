This page subscribes to the ESP32's MQTT topic over a WebSocket and renders what arrives — nothing is computed here that the device did not send. Health scores, anomaly detection and maintenance advisories are Objective 2; this is the telemetry layer proving itself end to end.
Each channel is plotted on its own axis: temperature, vibration, current and speed have unrelated units, so a shared scale would be meaningless.
